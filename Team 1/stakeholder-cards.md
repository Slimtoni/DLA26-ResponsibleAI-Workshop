# Stakeholder cards — Loan denial

### Card A: Loan officer (decision-maker)
- **Goal:** clear the daily queue, make defensible decisions, stay within compliance
- **Fear:** being held personally responsible for a rejection that later looks wrong; being slow
- **Wants to know:** "Can I trust this flag? What would my manager / a regulator think if I followed it?"
- **Entitled to:** a system whose output they can check; training on its limits; no automated decision without their sign-off

### Card B: Applicant (affected person)
- **Goal:** get the loan they need, or at least understand why it was refused
- **Fear:** being rejected for a reason they can't fix or contest; their data being used against them
- **Wants to know:** *why* specifically — which factor decided it; whether they can appeal; what they could do to improve their chances
- **Entitled to:** a meaningful explanation (AI Act / GDPR Art. 22), right to human review, right to contest

### Card C: Bank management
- **Goal:** reduce bad debt, reduce processing cost, keep the bank out of the press
- **Fear:** a systemic error pattern (e.g., rejecting self-employed applicants) that creates legal or reputational damage
- **Wants to know:** how well the system actually performs on *their* customers, not the vendor's benchmark
- **Entitled to:** (as deployer) audit trails, documentation, and the ability to switch off
