# Stakeholder cards — Production-line defect detection

### Card A: Line operator (decision-maker)
- **Goal:** keep the line moving, avoid false diverts that cost rework
- **Fear:** missing a defect that reaches the customer; being blamed when the line stops
- **Wants to know:** "Is this flag really a defect? What would it look like if I look at the part?"
- **Entitled to:** seeing the image that triggered the flag; a way to give feedback on false flags

### Card B: Customer (affected person)
- **Goal:** receive components that are safe and within spec
- **Fear:** a defect slipping through — especially if the component is safety-relevant
- **Wants to know:** (usually nothing directly) — but in a recall, they want to know why the defect was missed
- **Entitled to:** a quality process that is documented and auditable (industry standards, liability law)

### Card C: Factory management
- **Goal:** hit output targets and quality targets simultaneously
- **Fear:** the system creating a hidden bottleneck (too many diverts) or a hidden risk (too few)
- **Wants to know:** whether the pass/fail rate matches the known defect rate — a mismatch means the model or the process is broken
- **Entitled to:** (as deployer) documented accuracy, maintenance of the system, and a clear override log
