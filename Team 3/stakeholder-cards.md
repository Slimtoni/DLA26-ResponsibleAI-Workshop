# Stakeholder cards — HR shortlist ranking

### Card A: Hiring manager (decision-maker)
- **Goal:** hire 5 strong engineers in one round, without spending 2 days on CVs
- **Fear:** missing the best candidate because the score was wrong; being challenged on *why* they picked this 5
- **Wants to know:** what the score actually measures; whether the one-line summary matches the CV
- **Entitled to:** access to the full CV; the ability to rank in any order they choose

### Card B: Rejected applicant (affected person)
- **Goal:** a fair chance; a fair rejection
- **Fear:** being filtered out by something they can't see or contest — and never knowing it happened
- **Wants to know:** that a human actually read their application; a meaningful reason if not shortlisted
- **Entitled to:** GDPR Art. 22 rights, anti-discrimination protection; the "rejected good candidate" is invisible to the company — that is the design problem

### Card C: HR department
- **Goal:** manage the volume, defensible process, reduce time-to-hire
- **Fear:** a discrimination claim; the vendor's "success" metric encoding the company's own past biases
- **Wants to know:** how the model was trained, what "successful hire" meant, and whether the ranking correlates with protected characteristics
- **Entitled to:** (as deployer) vendor documentation, the right to audit, and a documented human-in-the-loop process
