# Stakeholder cards — Emergency-department triage

### Card A: Triage nurse (decision-maker)
- **Goal:** get the right patient to the right place at the right time
- **Fear:** overriding the system and being wrong; *or* following the system and missing a patient who "looked fine but wasn't"
- **Wants to know:** that their override is respected and not second-guessed; why the system recommends what it does
- **Entitled to:** professional autonomy; a system that supports, not replaces, clinical judgement; override without penalty

### Card B: Patient (affected person)
- **Goal:** be seen quickly if it's urgent; be told honestly if it isn't
- **Fear:** waiting too long because a machine said "standard" — or being rushed for a minor problem
- **Wants to know:** why they are being seen when they are being seen
- **Entitled to:** safe care; the system must not degrade the standard of care a human would provide

### Card C: Hospital management
- **Goal:** keep the ED flowing, meet wait-time targets, manage staff workload
- **Fear:** capacity crises; a pattern of under-triage that becomes a liability
- **Wants to know:** agreement rates, override patterns, and whether the system is actually reducing harm
- **Entitled to:** (as deployer) documented performance, incident reporting, and the ability to evaluate the system on *their* patient population
